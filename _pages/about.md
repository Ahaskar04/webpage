I'm a final year CS student at NTU. My research focuses on multi-VLA coordination for robotic systems (under review at ICLR 2026) under the guidance of Prof. Jianfei Yang and Prof. Stefano V. Albrecht. I'm also working on task and motion planning for cluttered pile retrieval, part of my final year project under Prof. Yoonchang Sung.

Alongside my research, I co-founded Solvd AI, an AI-powered physics tutoring platform, raising approximately $30,000 USD in pre-seed funding; the platform was piloted with Indian Ed Techs and went through NTU's iLAB accelerator program.

I'm applying for a direct PhD in robot learning starting Fall 2027.
